Getting started

New Survival worlds start with an empty inventory. Break a wood log, press E, and make planks in the 2 × 2 inventory grid. Four planks make a crafting table. Place it and right-click to open a 3 × 3 grid.

Craft a wooden pickaxe to mine stone for cobblestone. Use cobblestone for upgraded tools and a furnace. Torches give level 14 block light and stop hostile spawns wherever light is above zero. Carry food to recover health. Q drops one item and Shift + Q drops a stack.

An underwater breath meter appears only when the player’s head is submerged. Air lasts 15 seconds before drowning begins. A full day and night takes about 20 minutes of active play. A bed skips to morning once sunlight is low enough and there are no nearby hostile mobs.

Interfaces keep the world running

Inventory, crafting tables, chests, furnaces, electrical panels and machine interfaces stay live. Time, weather, generation, fuel use, processing and hazards continue while they are open. Movement and gameplay actions stay neutral while you use the interface.

Escape closes an open interface. During gameplay it opens the pause menu, which freezes simulation; settings opened from that menu also remain paused. Losing window focus opens the pause menu. Resume returns to the interface you were using.

Generation, wiring and distribution

Start with metalworking: smelt copper ore, then make wire and electrical blocks at the Metalworking Bench. See the exact bench recipes. Electricity is measured in W and kW; 1 kW is 1,000 W. There are no voltage settings, transmission losses or wire capacity limits.

SourceOutputRequirements
Solar PanelUp to 750 WAn access panel on the supply circuit, an output panel for distribution, daylight and an exposed sky. Regional precipitation reduces output to 30% of the daylight value. Night produces no power.
Coal Generator6 kW while fueledCoal or charcoal; each burns for 80 active seconds. Connect directly to an output panel without needing an access panel. Fuel burns while idle or disconnected.

Copper wire is placeable and connects automatically to neighboring wires and compatible ports, including vertical runs. Horizontal cable rests at ground level, meets low solar plates and rises against full-block terminals. Ordinary roofs, glass and snow above a solar panel block its sky exposure; thin wire does not.

Access panels and output panels are full blocks. Right-click an access panel to monitor its supply circuit. Feed the generator circuit into an output panel's rear, then connect machines to its marked front. Output panels and switches do not accept side connections. Other electrical blocks and electric machines connect on all faces.

Solar + access panel → output rear → output front → wire → switch rear → switch front → machines

For coal power, replace the solar/access pair with a coal generator. A power switch belongs downstream of the output panel and supports five reachable machines, including idle machines. Right-click to toggle its route. Use another branch or switch for additional machines. An independently wired live route can still power a machine when one route is off.

Multiple consumers share the available generation. If there is enough, each receives its requested power. Under shortage, reachable consumers share power in proportion to their requests; loops or overlapping panels do not duplicate generation. For example, a clear-noon 750 W solar circuit feeding two active 2 kW furnaces gives each 375 W, so each works at 18.75% speed.

Panel and generator interfaces report production, demand, consumption and available power. Available power is unused output, not stored energy. An idle or output-blocked machine requests zero. Generators also show fuel intake and remaining burn reserves. Changes are reflected at the electrical update cadence of 0.25 seconds.

Electric machines

Build all three at the Metalworking Bench using existing iron, copper, wire and earlier stations. Furnaces and cookers also need ceramic pots from the primitive brick kiln. Keep a brick kiln for firing pots: the electric glass kiln shapes glass instead.

MachineNominal demandInventory

The electric furnace processes the ordinary furnace's existing recipes in 2.5 seconds per item at full power, four times faster. Its four input and four output slots support mixed batches processed in input-slot order. Ordinary furnaces remain useful wherever there is fuel and no electrical infrastructure.

The electric glass kiln and electric cooker have product-selection arrows. Their menus show ingredients, rated power, current demand, actual allocated power and progress. A partially finished job locks product selection until it finishes or its ingredients are removed.

Processing speed follows the fraction of requested power received. At half power a job takes twice as long. Zero power or a full output pauses work without spending ingredients or discarding progress. Ingredients are consumed only when a complete result can be inserted. Output slots only allow collection.

Switching, changing generation, or disconnecting a cable updates the same shared network. Progress, selections, ingredients and completed products survive saving and chunk unloading. Machines outside the active simulation area stop until active again; there is no offline catch-up.

One fueled 6 kW generator can run all three machines at their nominal 4.9 kW combined demand, leaving 1.1 kW unused when no other machines request power. Browse electric processing recipes and timings.

Glass shapes, dyes and flower vases

The electric glass kiln accepts ordinary glass and all sixteen existing stained-glass colors. Select a product; the result keeps the input color. These times assume a full 2.5 kW allocation.

Glass inputOutputTime
12 glass slabs4 seconds

Slabs occupy the upper or lower half of a block. Two matching slabs join into a full-height double slab that drops both original slabs. Stairs support upright, upside-down and corner placement with collision following the steps. Matching-color glass faces are culled where they touch. Decorative tiles are full transparent blocks with an etched pattern.

Each new shape comes in clear glass and sixteen stained colors. Process matching stained glass in the kiln, or surround one existing dye with eight clear slabs, stairs, tiles or vases in the crafting table to color eight shapes. Existing pane crafting remains available. New shaped glass drops itself; ordinary glass blocks and panes still drop nothing when mined.

Right-click a vase with any flower to insert one flower. Right-click with an empty hand to retrieve it if inventory has room. Flower contents survive saving and chunk loading. Mining a filled vase drops the vase and its flower separately. See all clear and stained kiln recipes.

Creative mode

Creative players can fly, break blocks instantly, and place inventory blocks without using them up. Double-tap Space to toggle flight. While flying, hold Space to ascend, Shift to descend, or tap either Ctrl to toggle faster flight.

The Creative Items tab lets you find items by name. Click for one item or use Shift + click for a full stack. Items can be kept when switching to Survival. In either mode, F1 hides the HUD, F2 takes a screenshot, and F3 shows debug information.

Farming and breeding

Right-click grass or dirt with a hoe and plant wheat seeds, carrots or potatoes. Water within two blocks horizontally and one block vertically hydrates farmland. Harvest a fully grown crop for its mature yield. Ordinary tall grass has a 10% seed drop chance and double tall grass has a 20% chance.

Breed cows and sheep with wheat, pigs with carrots or potatoes, and chickens with wheat seeds. Leads can hold passive mobs to the player or tie them to a fence. Suitable enclosed fence pens protect passive mobs from despawning.

Mobs

Hostile mobs need effective light level 0 to spawn in suitable space. Peaceful removes hostiles and prevents new hostile spawns. Hostiles do not target a Creative player. The table lists each species’ normal drop type and maximum quantity.

Shearing a sheep gives wool without killing it. A recently sheared sheep needs time to regrow its wool. Adult chickens can also lay eggs.

Seasons and weather

Seasons is set on creation. Off keeps summer behavior and Nuclear Winter keeps winter behavior. On follows the computer’s local calendar. Spring starts March 20, summer June 20, autumn September 20 and winter December 20.

Winter slows outdoor crop growth and reduces yields. A properly enclosed greenhouse with a glass roof and artificial light allows normal growth and yields. Sheltered animals avoid the winter outdoor breeding penalty. Seasonal appearance can change existing vegetation without regenerating chunks.

Commands

Press T to open the command line and Tab for completion. Commands require a living player and a cheats-enabled world. Use underscores in multiword item names, or use the item ID shown in the catalog.
