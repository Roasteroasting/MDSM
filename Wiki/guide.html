Getting started

New Survival worlds start with an empty inventory. Break a wood log, press E, and make planks in the 2 × 2 inventory grid. Four planks make a crafting table. Place it and right-click to open a 3 × 3 grid.

Craft a wooden pickaxe to mine stone for cobblestone. Use cobblestone for upgraded tools and a furnace. Torches give level 14 block light and stop hostile spawns wherever light is above zero. Carry food to recover health. Q drops one item and Shift + Q drops a stack.

An underwater breath meter appears only when the player’s head is submerged. Air lasts 15 seconds before drowning begins. A full day and night takes about 20 minutes of active play. A bed skips to morning once sunlight is low enough and there are no nearby hostile mobs.

Creative mode

Creative players can fly, break blocks instantly, and place inventory blocks without using them up. Double-tap Space to toggle flight. While flying, hold Space to ascend, Shift to descend, or tap either Ctrl to toggle faster flight.

The Creative Items tab lets you find items by name. Click for one item or use Shift + click for a full stack. Items can be kept when switching to Survival. In either mode, F1 hides the HUD, F2 takes a screenshot, and F3 shows debug information.

Farming and breeding

Right-click grass or dirt with a hoe and plant wheat seeds, carrots or potatoes. Water within two blocks horizontally and one block vertically hydrates farmland. Harvest a fully grown crop for its mature yield. Ordinary tall grass has a 10% seed drop chance and double tall grass has a 20% chance.

Breed cows and sheep with wheat, pigs with carrots or potatoes, and chickens with wheat seeds. Leads can hold passive mobs to the player or tie them to a fence. Suitable enclosed fence pens protect passive mobs from despawning.

Mobs

Hostile mobs need effective light level 0 to spawn in suitable space. Peaceful removes hostiles and prevents new hostile spawns. Hostiles do not target a Creative player. The table lists each species’ normal drop type and maximum quantity.

Shearing a sheep gives wool without killing it. A recently sheared sheep needs time to regrow its wool. Adult chickens can also lay eggs.

Seasons and weather

Seasons is set on creation. Off keeps summer behavior and Nuclear Winter keeps winter behavior. On follows the computer’s local calendar. Spring starts March 20, summer June 20, autumn September 20 and winter December 20.

Winter slows outdoor crop growth and reduces yields. A properly enclosed greenhouse with a glass roof and artificial light allows normal growth and yields. Sheltered animals avoid the winter outdoor breeding penalty. Seasonal appearance can change existing vegetation without regenerating chunks.

Commands

Press T to open the command line and Tab for completion. Commands require a living player and a cheats-enabled world. Use underscores in multiword item names, or use the item ID shown in the catalog.
