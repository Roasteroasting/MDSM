 Gameplay guide

Starting in Survival, farming, animals, lighting and command syntax.

From clay and copper to electric processing

Fire clay balls into bricks for the Brick Kiln and ceramic pots. Smelt copper ore, then use the Metalworking Bench for wire, generators, panels and electric machines.

Wire a solar or coal-powered circuit, then use the electric furnace for batch smelting, the glass kiln for clear and stained shapes and flower vases, or the cooker for fast meals. The Mortar and Pestle provides dyes for all the new glass shapes.

Machine and inventory interfaces keep the world running. Learn how live interfaces and pausing work.
