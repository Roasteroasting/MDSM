World reference

Terrain generation

Infinite terrain is generated from the world seed and absolute coordinates as you explore. Wet shores, winding caves and the occasional jagged mountain come from different stages of the same generator.

World types and seeds

Infinite is the default. It streams 16 × 128 × 16 chunks, including terrain at negative X and Z. Legacy is the finite 1024 × 128 × 1024 option and uses its own generator. Older finite saves can still be loaded.

In Infinite worlds, generated chunks and player changes are saved in compressed region records. Seeded terrain features are computed in world coordinates so their geometry lines up across chunk edges. Seasonal decorations can depend on the season when an area first generates.

Biomes

Current Infinite generation samples separate multi-octave temperature and humidity fields. Cold regions become snowy forest, hot dry regions become desert, and the rest is temperate.

BiomeWhat to look for
TemperateGrass, trees, tall grass and mixed flower patches. Rare small-water shores can have crops.
DesertSand, sandstone layers, cacti 2–4 blocks tall and dead bushes. Less surface water and no rain.
Snowy forestTrees and thin snow layers. Regional precipitation falls as snow.

Land, rivers and shores

A three-dimensional noise density field builds rock and air. Broad elevation and relief fields give the world gentler regions, taller mountains, and occasional overhangs. Exposed land receives grass and dirt in temperate terrain and sand in suitable desert terrain.

Sea water fills low terrain near Y 63. Separate pond, lake and river features shape local beds and shores. Beach sand follows flatter shorelines, and natural sand can grade through a thin layer of sandstone before underlying stone. Clay appears as tapering clumps near shallow sandy water or in wet riverbed sediment.

Caves, deposits and ores

Seeded, branching tunnel walks carve through the solid terrain. Cave sections that would breach neighboring water are rejected. Deep cavities below Y 10 may contain lava. Underground sand and gravel form separate deposits, and ordinary ore veins have priority over those deposits.

Dungeons, vegetation and animals

Dungeons form where a room can connect to a cave. Look for cobblestone and mossy cobblestone walls and one to three chests. Loot can include iron equipment, iron ingots, food and farming supplies. Worlds with cheats enabled can use /locate dungeon.

Flower patches use nearby seeded anchors to mix several species. Spring adds peonies and violets across eligible temperate terrain. Rare patches of crops near small bodies of water in temperate terrain provide another route to carrots and potatoes. Passive animal generation also uses seed-based local placement.

The Far Lands

Around X or Z ±12,550,824, the terrain noise’s historical saturating lattice conversion begins extrapolating outside its normal range. The distorted terrain appears along an edge when one coordinate crosses the range and at a corner when both do. Generation continues beyond it.

Far Lands terrain generation is independent of the optional legacy visual precision setting. In a cheats-enabled Infinite world, use /tp 12550824 128 0 to test the eastern transition and F3 to read coordinates. Teleportation checks whether the destination is safe.
