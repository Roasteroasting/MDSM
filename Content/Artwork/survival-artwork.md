# Survival artwork

Original artwork generated with the built-in image-generation tool; no Minecraft assets were imported. Generated sheets were sampled at logical-pixel centers with nearest-neighbor placement. Prior terrain atlas pixels outside tiles 18–27 were compared and preserved exactly.

- [terrain.png](../Blocks/terrain.png): 18 Glass (clear cutout), 19 Iron Ore, 20 Coal Ore, 21 Diamond Ore, 22 Furnace top, 23 Furnace side, 24 Furnace front, 25 Chest top, 26 Chest side, 27 Chest front. Opaque block tiles have opaque alpha; Glass retains generated clear pixels.
- [survival.png](../Items/survival.png): editable 128×64 image, 8×4 grid of 16×16 sprites. Row 0: Iron Ingot, Coal, Flint, Diamond, Stick, Arrow, String, Charcoal. Row 1: four Wooden tools then four Stone tools; row 2: four Iron tools then four Diamond tools; each tier is pickaxe, axe, sword, shovel. Row 3 column 0: Bow. Remaining cells transparent.

The existing Content pipeline loads these PNGs. Neither launch nor build generates artwork. Terrain uses its existing content entry; the additional item sheet has its own entry. Pixel centers/point filtering remain in effect.

## Item sheet prompt

Create a production pixel-art inventory icon SPRITE SHEET, exactly 8 columns by 4 rows, equal square cells, transparent background, no grid lines/padding/labels. Every cell is a 16x16 logical pixel icon enlarged with perfectly crisp nearest-neighbor pixel blocks. Original early voxel sandbox artwork, never copy Minecraft assets. Row 1 left-to-right: silver iron ingot, black coal lump, gray flint shard, cyan diamond gem, brown wooden stick, single feathered arrow, coil of white string, dark charcoal chunk. Row 2 left-to-right: wooden pickaxe, wooden axe, wooden sword, wooden shovel, stone pickaxe, stone axe, stone sword, stone shovel. Row 3 left-to-right: iron pickaxe, iron axe, iron sword, iron shovel, diamond pickaxe, diamond axe, diamond sword, diamond shovel. Tools have clearly different T-pickaxe, angular axe blade, straight sword and shovel-spade silhouettes, all with brown handles, with tan wooden / gray stone / pale silver iron / saturated cyan diamond working heads. Row 4: curved wooden bow in first cell only, other seven cells completely transparent. Center each icon in its cell, distinct silhouettes, maximum 14x14 occupied pixels with one pixel transparent margin. Flat no perspective, limited palette, no glossy vector gradients, no text, no shadows beyond sprite. Whole sheet aspect ratio 2:1.

## Block sheet prompt

Create one original voxel-game texture sheet, exactly FIVE columns by TWO rows of equal square texture tiles edge-to-edge, NO gaps, NO labels, no perspective, no extra objects. Each square is a 16x16 logical pixel texture enlarged with nearest-neighbor crisp pixel blocks. Classic simple earthy pixel-art, original art never copy Minecraft assets. Row1 left-to-right: 1 clear glass pane with a thin pale blue-gray frame and tiny white diagonal corner glints, its central area truly transparent; 2 rough gray stone with scattered brown iron ore flecks; 3 rough gray stone with black coal ore flecks; 4 rough gray stone with rare bright cyan diamond flecks; 5 dark gray cobblestone furnace TOP, solid gray stone rim. Row2 left-to-right: 1 cobblestone furnace SIDE with coarse gray brick pattern; 2 furnace FRONT with a dark square unlit fire opening low down and a dark upper input slot; 3 chest TOP made of warm brown wooden boards with dark outer rim; 4 chest SIDE warm wood boards with dark reinforcing frame; 5 chest FRONT wood rim and centered tiny gold latch. All tiles opaque except the clear center of glass. No opaque white or black background inside the glass. Avoid realistic shading, text, halos, blur. Sheet aspect ratio 5:2.
