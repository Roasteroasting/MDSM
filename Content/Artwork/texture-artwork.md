# Milestone 4 texture artwork

The final editable asset is [terrain.png](../Blocks/terrain.png), a 256 x 256 PNG with 16 x 16 pixel tiles. Tiles 0-3 retain their original pixels. Tiles 4-14 contain the new bark, end grain, leaves, planks, cobblestone, sand, gravel, bedrock, tall grass, red flower, and yellow flower artwork. All other pixels were preserved, including transparent unused slots.

The new artwork was created once using the built-in `image_gen` tool with `transparent_background: true`, rather than the fallback CLI. The prompt below produced a 1448 x 1086 image arranged in four columns and three rows. During one-time asset preparation, the center of each logical texel was sampled into a 16 x 16 tile and copied into its assigned atlas slot. Material tiles were made opaque; leaf and plant alpha was thresholded at 128 to retain binary transparency. The top three rows of the plant tiles were cleared to remove stray edge artifacts. No preparation script runs during builds or gameplay.

Original tiles and pixels outside slots 4-14 were checked for exact equality after integration. Keep editing the final PNG directly; no generated source sheet or image-generation service is needed to build or run MDSM. See the [README](../../README.md#editing-block-textures) for tile assignments and the normal rebuild workflow.

## Final generation prompt

```text
Create ONE production pixel-art texture sheet for an early Minecraft Indev-inspired voxel game. It must be a precisely aligned 4-column by 3-row sheet, no outer margin, no gaps, no grid lines, no labels. Each cell is a flat square 16-by-16 logical-pixel texture; scale up the individual pixels sharply and uniformly so every texel remains a single flat color. Desired full canvas 1024x768 (4x3 aspect ratio); each texture square uses a logical 16x16 grid, enlarged 16 times. Not 3D cubes, not perspective drawings. Row 1 left to right: brown oak bark with vertical irregular grooves, tan oak end grain with blocky concentric rings, dark/medium green oak leaves with real transparent holes between leafy pixel clusters, warm brown horizontal wooden planks with dark seams and staggered joins. Row 2 left to right: medium gray cobblestone with irregular connected stone shapes and dark mortar, pale beige sand with sparse subtle ochre flecks, mixed gray/tan gravel with small angular pebbles, very dark gray bedrock with dense black/gray fractured patches. Row 3 left to right: a small upright tuft of tall grass rooted at bottom center on fully transparent background, a small red flower with a green stem and two leaves rooted at bottom center on fully transparent background, a small yellow flower with green stem and two leaves rooted at bottom center on fully transparent background, a completely transparent empty square. All opaque material cells fill their entire square, edge to edge. Only the leaves cell has scattered transparent holes (about 25 percent of pixels); leaves are an all-over pattern touching square edges, not a tree icon. Grass and flowers use transparent backgrounds with no ground, no box, no shadow, no checkerboard. Very simple recognizable earthy 16x16 pixel art, limited palette of flat colors. No anti-aliasing, no smooth gradients, no lighting effects, no decorative frame. Preserve true transparency for holes and plant backgrounds.
```
