# MDSM
MDSM (Mojang Don't Sue Me) is, at present, an attempt to recreate roughly the Alpha - Beta period of Minecraft, using mostly Codex for code and for some assets, while other assets are partially or fully hand-drawn. Game has some quirks, but I try to do as much polishing and optimization as I can. 
## Future plans
Eventually, I intend to add new mobs, new gameplay mechanics, new ores, new dimensions, and so on. The ultimate goal is for the game to grow as far away as possible from being a Minecraft clone and become much more original/derivative. As I've said, most of the code is AI-generated (still with my oversight; I can read but struggle to write C#), and I'm a tad ashamed that I'm not skillful enough to program it all myself. For that reason, I try to preserve the human aspect by deliberately ensuring that all of the Minecraft-independent features and game design are exclusively my ideas, and I intend to eventually replace all AI placeholder art in the game with my own hand-drawn art, even if it doesn't look as good.
## Some near-future game concepts (not even close to everything I have in mind)
* Skylands - angels, flying pigs, Old Testament sort of themes and possibly lore, ancient structures, Aztec temples, procedurally-generated large dungeons, Skypiea-esque. Current intent is to implement this as v3.0.0, or broken up into smaller updates.
* Electrical wiring - probably mostly 19th-century rather than anything excessively modern, but at the same time more explicitly electrical than something like redstone. Proper copper wiring, key elements of real-world electrical engineering (switches, logic gates, etc.), steam power, general steampunk influences, arc lamps and moonlight towers, waterwheels, and much much more. This will likely be v2.2.0 or v2.3.0.
* More biomes (jungles, savannas, wetlands, grasslands), and much wider varieties of both passive and hostile mobs (tigers, ostriches, deer, songbirds, lings, owls, rabbits, pheasants). Probably after wiring and before Skylands.
* More decorative blocks, generally - smooth stone, concrete, clay, iron blocks, other similar glaring content omissions. Will probably be added randomly, rather than having a dedicated update.
* Simple and complex metallurgy - probably having more primitive implementations for simple early-game stuff, along with more advanced mechanics for later on. The more advanced stuff will likely integrate with wiring. May have some early prototype stuff down by v2.3.0, with more full-fledged mechanics in v2.4.0.
